# The 12-week FAL class

Each weekday: **Review** (≈30 min) → **Daily Test** (15 Q: 10 new + 5 spaced review, 80% to unlock the next day).
Each Saturday: **50-question timed mock exam** (70% = pass, aim for 80%+).

| Target | When |
|---|---|
| Take real **TFM11** | End of week 5 |
| Take real **TFM12** | End of week 12 |
